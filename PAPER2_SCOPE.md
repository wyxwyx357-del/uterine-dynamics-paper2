# Paper 2 Scope

## Main question
Do a small number of local uterine dynamic measurements selected only from Paper 1 robustness evidence show associations with clinician-recorded uterine activity and clinical pregnancy?

## Analysis hierarchy
- Primary: dynamic measurement versus clinician-recorded forward/reverse peristalsis count.
- Secondary: dynamic measurement versus clinical pregnancy after prespecified clinical adjustment.
- Exploratory: incremental pregnancy prediction.

## Fixed boundaries
- Patient is the analysis unit.
- One formal record per patient.
- Same-examination linkage is required for the activity analysis.
- Forward and reverse activity remain separate unless an external definition justifies combining them.
- Clinical activity fields are treated as clinician-recorded counts; their observation duration is currently unknown.
- Paper 2 must not select the primary measurement using Paper 2 P values, pregnancy odds ratios, or AUC.
- No new direction, propagation, coordination, asymmetry, or deep-learning features are introduced here.
- Paper 1 algorithms are not rerun inside this repository; Paper 2 consumes frozen Paper 1 outputs.
